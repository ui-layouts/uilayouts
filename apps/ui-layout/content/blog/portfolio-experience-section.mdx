A portfolio experience section is more useful when it explains the scope and impact of each role, rather than acting as a timeline of logos. The Portfolio Experience block offers a starting layout.

## Structure the history

Use a consistent record for role, organization, start and end dates, responsibilities and one or two results. Distinguish personal contributions from team outcomes and mark overlapping freelance work accurately.

## Make entries scannable

Place the role and organization first, then dates and a short summary. Use a semantic list in reverse chronological order. Keep the date format consistent and do not rely on a decorative timeline to convey ordering.

## Link to supporting work

Where possible, link to public projects or case studies that demonstrate the contribution. Avoid disclosing private client work or confidential metrics; describe scope rather than inventing impact.

## Implementation example

The following focused example illustrates the technique. Use the linked component or block for the complete implementation.

```html
<ol>
  <li><h3>Design engineer</h3><p>Company · <time datetime='2025-01'>Jan 2025</time>–present</p><p>Describe your contribution and link to a relevant project.</p></li>
</ol>
```

## Before shipping

Verify dates, permissions and project links. Check the timeline with images disabled and on a small screen.

## Keep the heading semantic

Render the words as an ordinary heading. Position the particle canvas around or over it, marked decorative so it does not duplicate the title for assistive technology.

## Use a local particle budget

The documented examples use tsParticles. Choose restrained particle size, density and speed, and keep the effect bounded to the heading instead of the entire viewport.

## Protect readability

Avoid low-contrast text and rapid flashes. Provide a static heading for reduced motion or when the particle engine cannot load.

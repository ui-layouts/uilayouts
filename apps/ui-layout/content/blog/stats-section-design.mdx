A stats section communicates evidence, not decoration. A large number without a definition or time period can be visually impressive but difficult to trust.

## Choose the metric and denominator

Decide which outcome each number represents. For percentages include the population or comparison baseline; for totals include a reporting date or interval. Avoid presenting estimates as verified counts.

## Create a clear typographic hierarchy

Place the value next to its unit and label. Use tabular numerals when values update to prevent width shifts. Keep explanatory context visible rather than hiding it in a hover tooltip.

## Animate without changing meaning

A count-up can emphasize a value once, but the server-rendered content should still communicate the final number. Disable motion for users who request it and avoid repeatedly restarting when the section enters the viewport.

Accordions trade vertical space for progressive disclosure. A useful accordion opens the requested information without moving focus or making the answer depend on a decorative animation.

## Choose the interaction model

Decide whether several sections can be open simultaneously. A single-open accordion is useful for short menus, while independent sections are better for technical FAQs that readers compare.

## Animate intrinsic content carefully

A grid row can transition from zero to one fractional unit with an inner overflow-hidden wrapper. Avoid guessing a maximum height: long answers can clip or finish their animations too early.

## Keep controls semantic

Use a real button inside a heading and connect it to its panel using aria-expanded and aria-controls. When a panel is closed, ensure interactive descendants cannot receive focus. A native details element is another simple baseline.

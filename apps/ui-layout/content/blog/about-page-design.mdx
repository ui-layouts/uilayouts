An about section is an opportunity to explain why the organization exists and how it works, rather than repeat the hero headline. The Mission block provides a visual structure for a focused story.

## Lead with a point of view

State the problem you believe matters and why the organization is positioned to address it. Include a concrete example of the work instead of vague statements about innovation or excellence.

## Use imagery as evidence

Choose a team photo, process image or product artifact that supports the narrative. Give meaningful imagery useful alt text. Avoid generic stock photos that imply a team or workplace you do not have.

## Create a natural next step

Connect the story to a relevant team page, case study or contact action. Keep the narrative short enough to scan while making any linked detail easy to find.

## Implementation example

The following focused example illustrates the technique. Use the linked component or block for the complete implementation.

```html
<section aria-labelledby='mission-title'>
  <h2 id='mission-title'>Our mission</h2>
  <p>We help developers build expressive interfaces.</p>
  <a href='/components'>Explore our work</a>
</section>
```

## Before shipping

Check that claims and images are accurate and that headings make sense independently of the layout.
